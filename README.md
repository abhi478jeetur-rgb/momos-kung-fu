# Momo’s Kung Fu

A 60-second 3D animated short that renders **live in your browser**. There is no video file anywhere in it.

**Watch it:** https://abhi478jeetur-rgb.github.io/momos-kung-fu/

Momo the panda sells dumplings at the temple gate, but his heart belongs to kung fu. One clumsy flying kick, six airborne momos and one wise ox later, a legend begins.

## How it works
- The characters, props, temple and bamboo forest are all generated by a Blender Python script. Nothing is hand-modelled.
- The scene is exported as `assets/scene.glb`. Every moving part (124 objects, 8 cameras) is baked frame by frame into `assets/anim.bin.gz`.
- `app.js` (Three.js) poses the scene 24 times a second in sync with the soundtrack, cuts between the eight shot cameras, and draws shadows, sky and bloom in real time.
- Voices are neural text-to-speech. The music and sound effects are synthesised in Python.

## Controls
Space play/pause · ← → seek 5 s · F fullscreen · C captions · M mute. Quality menu: Auto / High / Low.

Needs a browser with WebGL 2. No AI video models were used.
