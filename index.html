How it was made

Modelled and animated in code

Every character, prop, temple and bamboo stalk is generated by a Blender Python script. No hand-modelling, no motion capture.

Rendered live with Three.js

The scene ships as a compact glTF model plus baked keyframes. Your browser poses all 124 moving parts and cuts between eight cameras 24 times a second.

Voices, music and sound

Narration and character lines use neural text-to-speech. The score and sound effects are synthesised in Python, then mixed to −14 LUFS.

No video generators

No AI video model touched this film. It is code, geometry and light, end to end.
